Plan v8 · sections and phases

Nyquist build plan

An offline, full-duplex voice agent for Hindi, Bengali, Marathi, Tamil, Telugu and Hinglish. A ~0.6B talker runs on the laptop's built-in graphics and an MoE thinker runs on its CPU cores. It is trained on free Kaggle T4s and runs on one Intel i3-10110U with no GPU. The work is split into 13 sections of 4 phases each, every phase has a week slot, and every dependency is written on the phase that needs it.

Compute

GPU budget by phase

Measured in T4-hours. One Kaggle session hour on 2× T4 counts as two T4-hours. One account gives about 60 T4-hours a week, which is about 1,440 over 24 weeks.

  • Honest read: the plan needs about 760–1,370 T4-hours, and one account supplies about 1,440. That leaves almost no room for re-runs. Synthetic dialogue speech (DA4) is the largest single cost, so measure its speed in week 6 before committing to it.
  • Kaggle allows one account per person. Three accounts are fine only if three teammates own them; stretching one person's quota across three accounts breaks Kaggle's terms and risks losing every notebook mid-project.
  • Course 120B slicing runs on the course cluster, not on Kaggle.
